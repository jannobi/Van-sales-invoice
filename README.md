# Business Management Application

Complete multi-panel business management system with Salesman, Admin, and Super Admin panels.

## Features

### Salesman Panel
- Party Management with live dues
- Item & Inventory Stock Management
- Live Sale & Purchase Invoice Generation
- Thermal/PDF Invoice Printing
- Godown-to-Van Stock Transfers
- Expense Logging
- Cash-in-Hand/Bank ledgers
- Monthly financial cycle with auto-reset

### Company Admin Panel
- Salesman Management
- Daily Routing & Scheduling
- Live Tracking
- Trial & Automated Billing

### Super Admin Panel
- Package & Gateway Customization
- Device Security & Session Tracking

## Technology Stack

- Backend: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Sequelize
- Frontend: React, Vite, TailwindCSS
- Real-time: Socket.io
- Payments: Stripe, PayPal, bKash, Nagad, Binance Pay
